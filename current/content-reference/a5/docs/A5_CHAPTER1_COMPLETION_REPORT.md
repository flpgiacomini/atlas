## Chapter spine

### 1885–1894 — Invention becomes a product
Benz, Daimler, Peugeot, Panhard & Levassor and the Benz Velo establish distinct paths from experimentation to repeatable vehicles.

### 1894–1906 — Competition becomes a development and publicity system
Paris–Rouen → Vanderbilt Cup → first Grand Prix de l'A.C.F.
The Entry model now works for both 1904 Vanderbilt and 1906 Grand Prix data.

### 1896–1908 — Companies and vehicle categories diversify
Daimler commercial truck, Renault, FIAT, Lancia, Rolls-Royce, Oldsmobile, Cadillac and Ford.

### 1901–1913 — Industrialization and standardization
Oldsmobile scale, Benz Velo volume, Cadillac interchangeable parts, Ford Model N/Model T and moving assembly line.

### 1914–1918 — Automotive factories become broader industrial assets
Benz and DMG aeroengine production, commercial-vehicle range changes, constrained luxury-car production and Renault taxi mobilization.
